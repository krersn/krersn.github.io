Privacy Policy — Yanik Online Card Game

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Yanik Online Card Game mobile application (the "App"), offered as a service by krersn Studio (the "Service Provider"). The service is provided "AS IS". The App is an online game; therefore, unlike the offline version, some of your data is processed on cloud servers so the game can function.

⚠️ 18+ Age Restriction: This app is intended for users 18 years of age or older only. The app contains simulated gambling mechanics (virtual chips, betting, per system). Virtual chips may be purchased with real money; however, virtual chips cannot be exchanged for real money, are non-refundable, and have no monetary value outside the app.

1. Information Collected and Purpose of Use

When you download and use the App, it may collect and process the following information:

  • Account identifier: When you first open the App, an account identifier is created on our server and a device key linked to that account is stored on your device (Android Block Store / backup is used so your account returns if you reinstall). No password or email is stored. You may optionally link your Google account or Google Play Games account, or create a recovery code; in that case only that service's player identifier (not your email) is linked to your account.
  • Profile information: The nickname you set in the game, the avatar you choose or the profile photo you upload, the cosmetic items you equip, and the public player ID shown to other players.
  • Social data: Your friends list, friend requests you sent/received, players you blocked, your online status and last-seen time.
  • Chat and message content: Public chat, table chat, player-created chat rooms and private messages are processed and stored so they can be delivered, shown as history, and to prevent abuse (profanity, link/phone sharing and spam filters).
  • Voice chat: If you turn on your microphone at a table or in a chat room, your voice is transmitted in real time to the players listening at that moment. Voice is never recorded or stored. The microphone is off by default and only used when you turn it on.
  • Game and statistics data: Number of games played and won, leaderboard scores, achievements, table records and your game progress.
  • Virtual economy records: Your virtual chip and diamond balance and its movements (table entries, winnings, chips sent between players, gifts, store purchases). These records are kept to show your transaction history and to prevent cheating and fraud.
  • Purchase records: When you make an in-app purchase, the purchase token, product ID and order ID provided by Google Play are processed for verification. Your credit/debit card or payment details are never seen or stored by the Service Provider; payments are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Device identifier for security: To prevent suspended (banned) users from returning with a new account, a one-way hashed digest of your device identifier is associated with your account. The raw device identifier never leaves the App.
  • Technical data: IP address during connection and server error/access logs (for security and troubleshooting).
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): When you watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may collect information such as the advertising ID (AD_ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, in-app interactions and diagnostics, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. You can change personalised ad preferences in the App's privacy options or your device settings.
The App does not collect your precise (GPS) location.
The App does not access your device contacts, calendar, SMS or photo gallery on its own. The microphone is used only when you turn it on for voice chat; a photo is used only if you choose it as your profile picture.
Your payment card details are never seen by the Service Provider; purchases are processed through Google Play.

2. Where Data Is Stored and International Transfer

For online features (account, friends, chat and chat rooms, gifts, statistics, virtual economy) to work, your data is stored and processed on servers operated by the Service Provider and hosted at a Hetzner Online GmbH data centre in Helsinki, Finland (European Union). Voice chat is relayed through the Service Provider's own voice server (LiveKit) on the same infrastructure.

Data related to Google Play, Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob is processed by Google on its own infrastructure and may be transferred outside your country; Google applies legal safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

3. Third Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (server hosting, EU): hetzner.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services / Sign in with Google: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (ads and consent): policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Games Services (player sign-in, leaderboards, achievements): policies.google.com/privacy

The open-source LiveKit software used for voice chat runs on the Service Provider's own server, not with a third-party company.

4. Player Content, Moderation and Messaging

The App offers public chat, table chat, player-created chat rooms, private messaging, voice chat and gift/chip sending. When using these features, please note:

  • Messages you send, your nickname, profile photo/avatar and cosmetic items can be seen by the recipients and by players in the room or at the table.
  • Your nickname, avatar and statistics (games played, wins, etc.) may be visible to other players on your profile card, in friends lists, table/lobby screens and leaderboards.
  • Messages are automatically filtered for profanity, link/phone/email sharing and spam.
  • When you report a player, the reported person's recent messages (public chat, chat rooms, table chat and private messages between you) are attached to the report as evidence and are visible only to authorised moderators.
  • For rule violations an account may be muted, removed from a chat room, or suspended temporarily or permanently.
  • You can report content or block a user with the in-app tools, or contact info@krersnstudio.com.
  • Some players marked with the 🤖 icon are virtual characters (software that replies automatically). Do not share personal information with virtual characters.

5. Data Sharing

The Service Provider does not sell or rent your personal information. Limited data sharing may occur only in the following cases:

  • With infrastructure and service providers necessary to deliver the service (server hosting: Hetzner; purchases, sign-in and ads: Google)
  • Google AdMob collecting the advertising ID, approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics while showing ads
  • When required by law (court orders, official requests)
  • When necessary to protect our rights, ensure user safety or investigate fraud

6. Data Retention Period

  • Account, profile, friends, statistics and virtual economy balance: Kept while your account is active; deleted immediately when you delete your account/data.
  • Private messages: The last 200 messages per conversation are kept; deleted (including the other party's copy) when you delete your account. You can delete your own message from both sides at any time.
  • Public chat messages: Kept for up to 30 days.
  • Chat room messages: The last 300 messages per room are kept; your messages are deleted and rooms you own are closed when you delete your account.
  • Voice chat: Never recorded.
  • Chip/diamond transaction records and purchase records: May be kept as long as necessary for anti-cheat, fraud prevention and accounting/legal obligations.
  • Reports: Kept because they are other users' safety records; if you delete your account your identity is anonymised in them.
  • Suspension (ban) records: If your account was suspended for rule violations, the ban record and device identifier digest are kept for security and fraud prevention for the duration of the ban (indefinitely for permanent bans), even if you delete your account.
  • Server logs and backups: Logs are kept for up to 30 days and database backups for up to 7 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Local data on your device: Removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Advertising ID / Play Games data: Subject to Google's own retention policies.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR and KVKK

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), you have the following rights:

  • To learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • To access the processed data and request a copy
  • To request correction or deletion of data
  • To object to data processing
  • To request data portability
  • To withdraw your consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@krersnstudio.com. Your request will be answered within 30 days at the latest.

8. Opt-Out and Data Deletion Rights

  • To delete your account and cloud data: use the account deletion option in the app or follow the steps on the Account & Data Deletion page.
  • To stop local data collection: uninstall the app from your device.
  • To disable personalized ads: Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of personalized ads"
  • To delete Play Games sign-in data: Play Games app → Settings → Account settings → Delete game data.

9. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. Because the app contains simulated gambling mechanics and real-money purchases, it is only suitable for users aged 18 and over.

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect data from users under 18. If we discover that a user under 18 has used the app, all related data will be deleted immediately. If, as a parent or guardian, you believe your child has used the app, please contact info@krersnstudio.com.

10. Security

The Service Provider applies physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the confidentiality of your information. All connections to the server are encrypted (HTTPS/WSS); account access is verified with a server-issued device key, servers are behind a firewall and the database is backed up daily. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

11. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are announced on this page and the "Last updated" date at the top is revised. Significant changes may be notified within the app. We recommend reviewing it regularly. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.

12. Consent

By using the App, you consent to the processing of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.
