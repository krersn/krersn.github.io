Account & Data Deletion

Applies to Yanik Online Card Game
Summary: Yanik Online Card Game creates an account on our server (no password or email; optionally linked to Google / Google Play Games) for online features. You can permanently delete your account and all linked data instantly from within the app, from the web deletion form, or by email (processed within 30 days).

Looking for Yanik Card Game Offline? The offline game does not create accounts; for local data deletion instructions, go to this page.

1. What Data Is Deleted?

Our servers (Hetzner, Helsinki, Finland — EU) store the following data for online features. Account deletion covers all of it:

  • Account, Player ID, display name, avatar and profile photo, equipped cosmetics
  • Chip/diamond balance, game statistics, achievements and rankings
  • Friends list, friend requests and block list
  • Private messages (including the other party's copy), your public chat and chat room messages; chat rooms you own are closed
  • Account links (Google / Google Play Games link, recovery code, device keys)

Voice chat is never recorded, so there is nothing to delete.

2. Deleting from Within the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the app.
  2. Go to Settings (⚙️) → Privacy & My Data.
  3. Tap "Delete My Data" and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and the data above. Deleted accounts, purchased chips and diamonds cannot be recovered.

3. Web Form or Email

If you no longer have the app, use the web deletion form with your Player ID, or send a request to the email below with your display name and approximate last play date.

Email info@krersnstudio.com
Subject "Account Deletion Request" / "Hesap Silme Talebi"

4. Processing Time and Retention

In-app and web-form deletions are immediate; email requests are processed within 30 days at the latest. The following may be retained:

  • Reports filed about you: kept as other users' safety records, with your identity anonymised.
  • Suspension (ban) records: if your account was suspended, the ban record and a hashed digest of your device identifier are kept for the ban period (indefinitely for permanent bans) for security and fraud prevention.
  • Chip/diamond transaction and purchase records: as long as needed for anti-cheat, fraud prevention and legal/accounting obligations. Card/payment data is held only by Google Play.
  • Server logs up to 30 days and database backups up to 7 days, then deleted automatically.

5. Local Data on the Device

Local data such as settings and cache is kept on your device. To delete it:

  • Uninstall the app: Android Settings → Apps → Yanik Online Card Game → "Uninstall".
  • Reset data (without uninstalling): Android Settings → Apps → Yanik Online Card Game → Storage → "Clear Data".

Note: Uninstalling the app or clearing local data does not delete your cloud account and data. For cloud data, follow the steps in section 2 or 3.

6. Google Play Games (Achievements & Leaderboard) Data

If you have signed in with Google Play Games Services, your achievements and leaderboard records are kept on Google's servers. To delete them:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app on your phone.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top right.
  3. Select Settings → Delete Play Games account.
  4. To delete data for a single game: Delete Play Games data → select the relevant game.

This process is managed by Google; krersn Studio has no additional control over your data.

7. Advertising ID (AD_ID) Data

Google AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to show personalized ads. You can reset this ID at any time:

  • Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID"
